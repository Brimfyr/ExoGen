# ADR 0008: The maps by shares, not cells

Status: accepted, 2026-11-22. Supersedes ADR 0007's decisions 2 (the liquid's depth), 4 (the shaded surface) and 5 (`mask_liquid`) as decisions 2 and 4 below state, and amends ADR 0006's decisions 2 and 3 (the cryosphere's maps drawn by shares with `cryosphere_share`, and the share of a pixel an ice hides) as decision 6 states. Amended the same day after the user's review of the drafts (F417): decisions 1 to 7 as they now read; the first reading is under "Amendment" below.

## Context

The user looked at the Earth-like's drafts (2026-11-21) and found its lakes, its coasts, its frost and its snow drawn as speckled single-pixel dots, the north-east continent's most of all, and asked whether the physics was limited by the map's resolution or whether some natural anti-aliasing was missing.

Each of those maps drew a threshold cell by cell, all or none: a cell under the liquid where its ground stood below the level (F26), a lake where its pit's water stood, ice or frost where its cell's own temperature kept it. The ground carries relief at the grid's own scale: on the Earth-like's coastal plains some 117 m at a pixel's scale, 82 m of it without the unresolved craters' roughness (F54), the rest the other processes' relief at that scale. Where the ground hovers near a threshold, that relief decided each cell's side on its own, and the margin speckled: 228 lone cells of sea and 268 of land on the Earth-like's draft, 1,961 lone lakes over a tenth of the deepest on Titan's, 262 lone cells in the Earth-like's caps' ice, the snapshot's snow edge a scatter. The relief at a cell's scale deciding where a margin lies is noise placing, against PLAN principle 16 (physics places, noise realises). The user asked for the maps to be drawn by shares.

## Decision

1. The maps read the cells about each cell as samples of the ground within it (F416, F417): over a Gaussian of `RELIEF_SPREAD_CELLS`, two cells, about it. Two cells is the finest relief the grid draws as shape, twice the finest the relief at the cell's scale reaches (F54's octaves to two cells). A kind's share of a cell is its cells' mean there as ones and noughts, the model's own decision on each cell, none under half the 8-bit mask's least step. A quantity of one kind's over that kind's share is a cell's own where the cell is of that kind, and where not the mean of the kind's cells about it; a map of it holds the share times that, its mean over the cell. A cell a kind covers whole keeps its own: only the margins are drawn by shares, and a quantity stands above none exactly where its share does.
2. `mask_liquid` holds the sea's share of each pixel, 0 to 255 for 0 to 1; `liquid` the sea's mean depth over each pixel, its depth over the share it covers this over that share, a cell under the sea its own depth; both read between cells.
3. `lakes` holds the lakes' mean depth over each cell by the same rule; read between cells, where it was nearest-cell.
4. `snapshot` draws each kind over its own share of each cell: the sea's water as it shows over the sea's share, under the sea's ice; the land's ground under the land's ice over the rest. The water of a wet cell is its own floor under its own depth, and of a dry cell the mean of its wet cells' colours about it, so that a shore shows its shallows; the land's ground is a dry cell's own albedo and a wet cell's the mean of its dry cells' about it; each ice at the snapshot's phase, its cover and its colour, its mean over its own kind's cells about the cell. `shaded` lights the sea's flat level over the sea's share of each cell and the land's ground over the rest, a dry cell's own and a wet cell's its dry cells' mean.
5. `ice`, each `ice_<species>`, `frost` and `sea_ice` hold their means over each cell by the same rule, a cell they cover their own.
6. The ices that come and go (ADR 0006) are drawn by the same rule, so that an engine drawing them at any phase draws their margins by shares (F417, the user's choice): `cryosphere_share` holds the share of each pixel the cells an ice lies on cover; `cryosphere_window` and `cryosphere_ice` hold a cell's own window and ice where an ice lies on it, and at the margin the nearest such cell's, no mean blending a window round the year's end; `cryosphere_thickness` holds the ice's thickness at its peak over that share, a cell's own where an ice lies on it and the mean of those about it at the margin. An engine's ice hides that share times 1 - exp(-m / cover mass) of a pixel (the sidecar's evaluation).
7. The model's own fields are unchanged: the liquid the climate and the rivers read, the caps, the frost, the sea's ice and the cryosphere's windows; the maps draw them.

## Consequences

- A pipeline that wants a binary coast thresholds `mask_liquid` at a half, which lies where the model's own coast lies, smoothed over the samples' Gaussian; on a plain the relief at the cell's scale speckles, within a cell of where the plain's shape crosses the level (the shares' test).
- An engine composing the snapshot itself draws the liquid over `mask_liquid`'s share of each pixel at `liquid`'s depth over that share, and the ground bare over the rest (the sidecar's evaluation); the mean depth drawn over the whole pixel paints a pixel a fifth wet as dark as the open sea.
- An engine that sets its own sea level and draws its coast from `height` alone gets the cells' edge, all or none.
- The maps soften every margin over some two cells, a sharp one (a cliff's coast, a cap's wall) too: from a tenth to nine tenths of a straight coast's share over some five cells. Within a kind nothing is softened: the sea's depths, a cap's thickness, the lakes' depths, the frost, the sea's ice and the ices' windows and thicknesses stand as the model left each cell they cover, and every map not named here (the height, the albedo, the climate's, the rivers') is the model's own, cell by cell.
- An engine that reads the ices' windows without `cryosphere_share` draws their margins whole, a cell or so past where the model's cells stop.
- The model's decisions on its own grid still read each cell all or none: the hypsometric fill's hollows under the level (F26), the frost's and the caps' cells. The maps no longer show that relief deciding them; the model's own decisions there wait for the processes that wear the relief down (the loop's erosion, creep).

## Amendment

The first reading (F416) took a cell's ground as a normal distribution about its mean over the same Gaussian, spread by the root mean square of the ground about that mean, and drew the sea's share and mean depth from it; the snapshot drew each cell's water at that mean depth over the whole cell, each ice by its mean over the cells of both kinds, and each cell's own albedo under both. The user found on its drafts (2026-11-22) coasts drawn as a thin line of water inside the land, a strip of land and the sea, and TRAPPIST-1e's frozen seas edged in open water on its night side, then, with the ices drawn by kind, its iced continents in dark outlines where they met the sea's ice. Where the ground's shape is steep and resolved (a mountain coast, a margin's front), the spread was the shape's, not the relief unresolved within a cell: its tail put valleys 150 to 500 m over the level a seventh under the sea, and the mean, pulled down by a sea deeper than its land is high, drew the coast up the land. The water at a mean depth over a whole cell drew a fifth wet as dark as the open sea. Each ice's mean over both kinds thinned a frozen sea's ice along its coast by the land's bare cells, a snowy land's by the open sea's. And a coastal sea cell's own albedo, its floor's sediment, drawn under the land's share showed the floor bare where the land's caps stood white. Read as samples, the share has no tail past the cells and no pull from the depth, and each kind is drawn over its own share with its own ground. The user then asked why the shallow seas' teal had gone from the coasts: the depth's mean over the cells about each cell, F416's and the samples' first drawing, drew a shelf or a bank in shallow water beside a deep sea at the deep sea's depth, the Earth-like's wet pixels under 30 m 1,168 on its draft where its cells drawn all or none had 6,695, and blurred the depth, the caps' thickness and the frost everywhere they lay; a cell a kind covers now keeps its own, and a shore's water is the mean of its wet cells' colours, not the colour of their mean depth, which drew a shore beside a shelf 10 m deep as dark as the deep sea beyond it. And the user found the land's seasonal ice still speckled in the maps an engine draws it from, which this record had kept cell by cell (its first decision 6); asked, the user chose to draw them by shares too (decision 6 as it now reads).
