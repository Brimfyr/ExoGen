# ADR 0009: The maps agree at every margin

Status: accepted, 2026-11-22. Amends ADR 0007's decision on the albedo (the ground itself, under its liquid too) and its evaluation, and ADR 0008's decisions 2 and 4 as decisions 2 to 5 below state.

## Context

ADR 0008 drew the sea, the lakes and the ices by shares, each margin softened over the cells about it read as samples of the ground within it. The user then asked (2026-11-22) that every map that would benefit from it be drawn alike, "to avoid this unnatural look and to keep all maps consistent", and that "everything that could cause a mismatch" be looked at, asking whether the height map needed it now that the coasts were drawn by shares. An engine composes the maps together: a flat sea over the height, the sidecar's evaluation from the albedo, the masks and the depths, the ices over the ground, rivers over the land. Measured on the F417 drafts:

- The height crosses the sea's level where each cell's own ground does: 224 lone pixels of sea and 334 of land on the Earth-like where mask_liquid's half has 16 and 28, the two disagreeing on 2,448 pixels, every one of them at the coasts' margins; TRAPPIST-1e 1,664, Titan 124. A sea drawn as a flat surface over the height brought the speckled coast back.
- The sidecar's evaluation drew the land's share of a coastal pixel in albedo.exr's colour, at a wet cell the floor's sediment the model laid there: the dark outline ADR 0008 took out of the snapshot. And it drew the water from one albedo at the pixel's mean depth, the colour of the mean depth the shallows' teal lost.
- The lakes were drawn by shares with no share given: an engine drew every pixel the depth stood above none on, Titan's lakes over 375,453 pixels where its cells drawn all or none covered 157,600. The snapshot drew no lakes at all (F277's limitation): the Earth-like's lakes cover an eighth of its land.
- The rivers stopped where the model's last dry cell stood: 24 of the Earth-like's 103 mouths of more than 1,000 m³/s a pixel short of the drawn sea, 14 of TRAPPIST-1e's, at narrow inlets' heads where the sea is drawn under half of its cells.
- The aridity, the land's and the open water's, read cell by cell: 4,037 of the Earth-like's 10,169 coastal pixels on the land's side read nearer the sea's.
- The temperatures, the surface's age and the units, each cell's own of its kind, the floor's or the land's, stood at the cells the relief at their scale set apart under a pixel drawn as the other kind.
- Drawn in the snapshot, the lakes stood on the caps' ice: TRAPPIST-1e's over 51,286 of the 56,604 pixels they cover a twentieth of or more, at 231 K on the median over 2,306 m of ice, a blue stipple on its ice sheets; the Earth-like's over 11,428 of 39,369. `lakes` and `mask_lakes` had drawn them there all along, water on the ice the caps' map lays.
- Each pixel read over its footprint (decision 8), the height crossed the sea's level up to a pixel off mask_liquid's half along the coasts: a pixel the sea covers over half, its shelf's floor some metres under the level and the land beside it some hundreds over, stood over the level, on 617 of the Earth-like's pixels, 866 of TRAPPIST-1e's and 261 of Titan's, all but 14 within a pixel of the mask's half.

## Decision

1. A map drawn cell by cell whose values belong to a kind of cell, the sea's floor, a lake's or the land, is drawn so that its kinds divide where their shares are even (F419): a cell on the lesser side of the cells about it, between the sea and the land and then between a lake and the land, takes the greater side's mean over the cells about it, or, where values cannot be blended (a unit's id), the nearest cell's on the greater side; every other cell keeps its own. The height (its ground and the caps' ice on it), the temperatures, the surface's age and the units. Not a smoothing: a cell keeps its own wherever its kind holds the greater share about it, a cliff's coast its cliff, and only the cells the relief at the cells' scale set apart change. The height then crosses the sea's level where mask_liquid's share is a half, and each lake's where mask_lakes' is.
2. The lakes are given their share, `mask_lakes`, as the sea's is in mask_liquid, their depth standing above none exactly where it does (F420); the snapshot draws them over their share, their water over their floor at their own depth by the liquid's optics, under the land's ice, which lies on the land and its frozen lakes alike.
3. `albedo` holds the land's ground over the land's share of each cell, neither sea nor lake: a land cell's own, a cell under the sea or a lake the mean of the land cells' about it, and under the open sea past them the floor's own (F420). `water` holds the liquid as it shows over its share, the sea's and the lakes': a cell under it its own floor under its own depth, another the mean of the colours of those about it. The snapshot is composed from them, cell by cell, as the sidecar's evaluation composes them: the pixel s (W under the sea's ice) + (1 - s) (L under the land's ice), L = (l W + (1 - s - l) G) / (1 - s), s and l the sea's and the lakes' shares, W the water, G the land's ground.
4. The rivers are drawn where the land is drawn over more than half of each cell (F421): a river cell the water is drawn over half of or more is cut, and a river cell beside the water carries its river on through the water's cells, toward the greater share, until it reaches one the water is drawn over half of.
5. The aridity is drawn over the land's share as the albedo is (F420): the land's index at a coastal pixel's land, the open water's past the land.
6. The climate on the finer grid is the model's, not the maps', and reads the ground's shape the grid resolves, the cells about each cell its samples as the maps read them, where each cell's own relief at the grid's scale placed its margins cell by cell: the rain over the relief (F422), its slope and height theirs; the temperatures, the frost, the seasons' ices and the land's snow, and where a working liquid's caps melt (F423), the land's shape at each land cell the mean of the land cells about it, the sea's cells their own. The poles' shadows read the ground itself, whose craters hold them.
7. Maps of the whole surface that no kind of cell divides (the precipitation, the wind, the clouds) are the model's own, cell by cell.
8. Every map is read over each pixel's footprint (F424): where the pixels are wider than the cells, some four across on a draft 1,024 pixels wide over a grid of 1,024 cells a face, a pixel read between the cells at its centre alone took the few cells nearest it, and the cells' own variation within a kind (the water's colour over a lowland its cells flood shallow and deep by turns, the snow's thickness) aliased into the pixels as speckle. Each pixel is now the field's mean over its footprint, read between the cells at as many points across it each way as cells cross it; a pixel a cell wide or less is read at its centre alone, as before. Maps whose values cannot be blended (an ice's window and index, the units) are read at the cell under each pixel's centre, the rivers by the most of the cells in each pixel.
9. A basin brought no water holds none (F425), the model's: a lake's cells flood only while water is left to flood them. A hollow of the caps' ice, which sheds none and keeps the year's snow as its ice, its surface losing none, had stood full to its pass, a lake of no water; the caps' ice now holds none, and the lakes, the rivers and the snapshot agree with the caps.
10. Each pixel of the height is the mean over its footprint of the cells on its own greater side (F426): the sea's where mask_liquid's share of it is a half or more, a lake's where mask_lakes' is, else the land's. Read over its whole footprint (decision 8), a coastal pixel the sea covers over half held its shelf's floor and the land's ground beside it together and stood over the level; the height now crosses the levels where the masks' shares are a half at the pixels as at the cells.

## Consequences

- An engine that draws the sea as a flat surface over the height draws its coast where mask_liquid's share is a half, pixel for pixel, and a lake's where mask_lakes' is; one that draws by the shares draws the same margins softened.
- An engine that follows the sidecar's evaluation reproduces the snapshot, its coasts and lakes' shores without the floor's outline and its shallows' colour as the snapshot shows it.
- `albedo` under the open sea is the floor's own and near the coasts the land's ground: an engine that sets its own sea level reads the land's ground under a sea it raises within some cells of the old coast, not the floor the model laid there.
- A map read alone shows its kinds divided where the masks divide them; the lone cells the relief at the cells' scale set on the other side are drawn as their side's, as the masks and the snapshot draw them.
- The model's own fields are unchanged but for the climate on the finer grid (F422, F423), the caps' profile over the ground under them (F418) and the lakes, none on the caps' ice (F425), the model's.
- A cap's outline is still its places' smooth contour on the climate's grid, a blob at the map's scale (the user, 2026-11-22): placed by the vapour its ice holds on each cell's own ground, Mars's near-flat polar traps let a few kelvin outrank its places and scattered it (F423, tried); its outline over the terrain waits for its own mass balance on the finer grid.
